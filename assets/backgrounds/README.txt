THEME: Regular / Video
======================
A small switch (bottom-left) lets visitors choose  Regular  (plain dark blue)  or  Video  (moving background).
The choice is remembered in the browser.

default.mp4        -> theme video used on EVERY page (replace it with your own video, keep the name)
news.mp4           -> only for the News & Highlights page  (overrides default.mp4)
education.mp4 / skills.mp4 / projects.mp4 / certificates.mp4 / contact.mp4 / about.mp4 / home.mp4  -> same idea
(.webm works too.)  Tall clip for phones: news-mobile.mp4, skills-mobile.mp4 ...

Tips: 10-20 s loop, no sound needed (muted), 1280x720 or smaller, under ~5 MB.
Darkness / blur: data.json -> "backgrounds" -> "overlay" (0 = clear video, 1 = black) and "blur" (pixels).
